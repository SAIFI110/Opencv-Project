(A) The direction of wave propagation ✓
(B) The direction of particle oscillation
(C) The location of compression
(D) The location of rarefaction
Correct Option: (A) • The red arrow labeled 'حركة الموجة' indicates the direction in which the wave is moving, which is from left to right.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain how compressions and rarefactions contribute to the propagation of a longitudinal wave.
Explanation: In a longitudinal wave, compressions occur where particles of the medium are close together, resulting in higher pressure and density. Rarefactions occur where particles are spread apart, leading to lower pressure and density. As the wave propagates, the alternating regions of compression and rarefaction allow energy to transfer through the medium, causing the wave to move forward while the individual particles oscillate back and forth in the direction of the wave's movement.

📝 Question 1: Short Question (سؤال قصير)
What is the definition of wavelength in a transverse wave?
Answer: Wavelength in a transverse wave is the distance between two consecutive crests or troughs.
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
In the diagram, how is the wavelength of a longitudinal wave measured?
(A) Distance between the centers of two consecutive compressions ✓
(B) Distance between two consecutive crests
(C) Distance between two consecutive troughs
(D) Distance between two consecutive rarefactions
Correct Option: (A) • The wavelength of a longitudinal wave is measured as the distance between the centers of two consecutive compressions, as indicated in the diagram.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain how the wavelength differs between transverse and longitudinal waves.
Explanation: In transverse waves, the wavelength is measured as the distance between two consecutive crests or troughs, reflecting the vertical displacement of the wave. In contrast, in longitudinal waves, the wavelength is measured as the distance between the centers of consecutive compressions, which represent areas of high pressure, and rarefactions, which represent areas of low pressure. This difference arises from the nature of how energy is propagated in each type of wave.

💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain how the amplitude of a longitudinal wave is affected by the medium's density.
Explanation: The amplitude of a longitudinal wave is influenced by the density of the medium because greater density allows for more pronounced compressions and rarefactions. In denser materials, the particles are closer together, which enhances the wave's ability to transmit energy through stronger compressions, resulting in a larger amplitude.

📝 Question 1: Short Question (سؤال قصير)
What is the law of reflection?
Answer: The law of reflection states that the angle of incidence is equal to the angle of reflection.
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
In the diagram, what is the relationship between the angle of incidence and the angle of reflection?
(A) They are equal. ✓
(B) The angle of incidence is greater.
(C) The angle of reflection is greater.
(D) They are unrelated.
Correct Option: (A) • According to the law of reflection, the angle of incidence is always equal to the angle of reflection.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain why the angles of incidence and reflection are equal in the law of reflection.
Explanation: The angles of incidence and reflection are equal because of the way light behaves when it strikes a reflective surface. When a light ray hits a surface, it reflects off at the same angle it arrived, maintaining the principle of symmetry in the reflection process. This is fundamental to the behavior of waves, including light, and is described mathematically in the law of reflection.
