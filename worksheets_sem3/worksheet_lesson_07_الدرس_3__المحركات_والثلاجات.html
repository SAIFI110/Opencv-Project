📝 Question 1: Short Question (سؤال قصير)
What is the function of the fuel injector in an internal combustion engine?
Answer: The fuel injector sprays fuel or an air-fuel mixture into the combustion chamber.
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based

(A) حاقن الوقود (Fuel Injector) ✓
(B) صمام الحقن (Injection Valve)
(C) المكبس (Piston)
(D) الأسطوانة (Cylinder)
Correct Option: (A) • The حاقن الوقود (Fuel Injector) is specifically designed to inject fuel into the combustion chamber, as shown in the diagram.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain how the piston movement affects the combustion process in a four-stroke engine.
Explanation: In a four-stroke engine, the piston moves down during the intake stroke, creating a vacuum that draws in the air-fuel mixture. This mixture is then compressed as the piston moves up during the compression stroke, leading to ignition and combustion. The downward movement of the piston during the power stroke pushes the crankshaft, converting the energy from combustion into mechanical work.

📝 Question 1: Short Question (سؤال قصير)
What is the function of the spark plug in an internal combustion engine?
Answer: The spark plug ignites the air-fuel mixture in the combustion chamber.
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
Which component connects the piston to the crankshaft in a four-stroke engine?
(A) Connecting Rod ✓
(B) Spark Plug
(C) Cylinder Liner
(D) Intake Valve
Correct Option: (A) • The connecting rod links the piston to the crankshaft, allowing the piston’s movement to turn the crankshaft.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain the role of the compression stroke in a four-stroke engine cycle.
Explanation: During the compression stroke, the piston moves upward in the cylinder, compressing the air-fuel mixture. This increases the mixture's temperature and pressure, making it more combustible when ignited by the spark plug, which leads to an efficient power generation in the engine.

📝 Question 1: Short Question (سؤال قصير)
What is the function of the exhaust valve in a four-stroke engine?
Answer: The exhaust valve allows exhaust gases to escape from the cylinder after combustion.
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
Which component is indicated as the exhaust valve in the diagram?
(A) صمام العادم ✓
(B) صمام السحب
(C) شمعه الإشعال
(D) المكبس
Correct Option: (A) • The exhaust valve is labeled as 'صمام العادم' in the diagram, indicating its function to release exhaust gases.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain how the movement of the piston affects the operation of the exhaust valve during the exhaust stroke.
Explanation: During the exhaust stroke, the piston moves upwards, creating a decrease in pressure inside the cylinder. This pressure difference causes the exhaust valve to open, allowing the exhaust gases to escape from the cylinder. The upward movement of the piston is crucial for expelling the gases efficiently.

📝 Question 1: Short Question (سؤال قصير)
What is the function of the compressor pump in a refrigeration system?
Answer: The compressor pump circulates and compresses the refrigerant within the system.
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
Which component is responsible for controlling the flow of refrigerant in the refrigeration system?
(A) صمام التمدد (Expansion Valve) ✓
(B) مضخة ضاغطة (Compressor Pump)
(C) ملفات التكثيف (Condenser Coils)
(D) وحدة التجميد (Freezing Unit)
Correct Option: (A) • The صمام التمدد (Expansion Valve) regulates the flow of refrigerant, allowing it to expand and cool before entering the freezing unit.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain how the refrigeration system expels heat from the cooling compartment.
Explanation: The refrigeration system expels heat by using the condenser coils, which are located outside the cooling compartment. As the refrigerant vapor enters the condenser coils, it releases heat to the surrounding air, causing it to condense into a liquid. This process effectively removes heat from the cooling compartment, maintaining a low temperature inside the refrigerator.
