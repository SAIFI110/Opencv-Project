What are the two regions of sound wave propagation illustrated in the diagram?
Answer: The two regions are compression (تضاغط) and rarefaction (تخلخل).
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
What does the region labeled 'تضاغط' in the diagram represent?
(A) Compression ✓
(B) Rarefaction
(C) Vibration
(D) Sound frequency
Correct Option: (A) • The region labeled 'تضاغط' translates to 'compression', indicating a higher density of air particles.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain how striking the drum creates sound waves in the air.
Explanation: Striking the drum causes its membrane to vibrate, creating areas of compression where air particles are pushed closer together, followed by areas of rarefaction where air particles are spread apart. This alternating pattern of compression and rarefaction propagates through the air as sound waves.

💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain how the vibration of the drumhead creates sound waves.
Explanation: When the drumhead vibrates, it compresses the air particles directly above it, creating areas of high pressure (compression) and low pressure (rarefaction). This alternating pattern of pressure travels through the air as sound waves, allowing us to hear the sound produced by the drum.

Which sound is associated with a decibel level of approximately 120 dB?
(A) Airplane Takeoff ✓
(B) Household Sounds
(C) Falling Leaves
(D) Cat's Purr
Correct Option: (A) • An airplane takeoff is typically around 120 dB, indicating a very loud sound.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain why sounds at or above 120 dB can be harmful to human hearing.
Explanation: Sounds at or above 120 dB can cause immediate damage to the hair cells in the inner ear, leading to hearing loss. Prolonged exposure to such loud sounds can result in permanent damage, as the intense pressure can overwhelm the auditory system, causing pain and discomfort.

📝 Question 1: Short Question (سؤال قصير)
What is the principle illustrated by the diagram regarding sound waves?
Answer: The diagram illustrates the reflection of sound waves within an enclosed space.
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
What does the Arabic text 'اتجاه الصوت' indicate in the diagram?
(A) Direction of sound ✓
(B) Source of sound
(C) Reflection of sound
(D) Absorption of sound
Correct Option: (A) • The Arabic text 'اتجاه الصوت' translates to 'direction of sound', indicating the path of sound waves from the source.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain how sound waves reflect in an enclosed space as shown in the diagram.
Explanation: Sound waves propagate outward from the source and hit the walls, ceiling, and floor of the enclosed space. Upon hitting these surfaces, the waves bounce back, changing direction. This process of bouncing off surfaces is known as reflection, and it can occur multiple times, allowing sound to travel throughout the space.
