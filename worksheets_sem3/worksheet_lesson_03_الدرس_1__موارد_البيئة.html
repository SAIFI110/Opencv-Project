What type of energy is represented by the water held behind the dam?
Answer: Potential energy of water.
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
What happens to the potential energy of water as it flows down from the dam?
(A) It is converted into kinetic energy. ✓
(B) It remains unchanged.
(C) It is lost as heat.
(D) It becomes electrical energy directly.
Correct Option: (A) • As the water flows down from the dam, its potential energy is converted into kinetic energy, as indicated in the diagram.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain how the kinetic energy of water is utilized in hydroelectric power generation.
Explanation: The kinetic energy of the flowing water is used to rotate a turbine. As the water flows through the turbine, it causes the turbine blades to spin, which in turn drives a generator to produce electricity. This process effectively converts the kinetic energy of the water into electrical energy.

📝 Question 1: Short Question (سؤال قصير)
What is the primary energy source used in a nuclear power plant?
Answer: The primary energy source is nuclear energy derived from atomic reactions.
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
What process occurs after the nuclear energy is converted into thermal energy in a nuclear power plant?
(A) The thermal energy heats water to produce steam. ✓
(B) The thermal energy is stored in batteries.
(C) The thermal energy is released into the atmosphere.
(D) The thermal energy is converted directly into electricity.
Correct Option: (A) • The thermal energy heats water, converting it into steam, which is essential for driving the turbine.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain how the conversion of nuclear energy to kinetic energy occurs in a nuclear power plant.
Explanation: In a nuclear power plant, nuclear energy is first generated in the reactor vessel through atomic reactions. This energy is then used to heat water, converting it into steam. The steam, which possesses kinetic energy, is directed towards a turbine. As the steam expands and moves through the turbine, it causes the turbine blades to rotate, thus converting the kinetic energy of the steam into mechanical energy, which is then transformed into electrical energy by a generator.
