📝 Question 1: Short Question (سؤال قصير)
What are the two components of an electromagnetic wave?
Answer: The two components of an electromagnetic wave are the electric field and the magnetic field.
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
Which field oscillates vertically in an electromagnetic wave?
(A) Electric field (المجال الكهربائي) ✓
(B) Magnetic field (المجال المغناطيسي)
(C) Both fields oscillate vertically
(D) Neither field oscillates vertically
Correct Option: (A) • The electric field (المجال الكهربائي) is represented by the blue sinusoidal wave and oscillates vertically, as indicated in the diagram.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain why the electric and magnetic fields in an electromagnetic wave are perpendicular to each other.
Explanation: The electric and magnetic fields in an electromagnetic wave are perpendicular to each other due to the nature of electromagnetic radiation. When one field reaches its maximum amplitude, the other field is also at its maximum, and they oscillate in such a way that they maintain a 90-degree angle. This perpendicular arrangement allows for the efficient propagation of the wave through space, as described by Maxwell's equations.

📝 Question 1: Short Question (سؤال قصير)
What is the first part of the eye that light passes through?
Answer: The cornea.
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
Which part of the eye is responsible for refracting light to focus it on the retina?
(A) Lens ✓
(B) Pupil
(C) Cornea
(D) Retina
Correct Option: (A) • The lens is responsible for refracting light rays to focus them on the retina, as indicated in the diagram.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain how light is focused on the retina in the human eye.
Explanation: Light enters the eye through the cornea, which begins the focusing process. It then passes through the pupil, where the amount of light is regulated. After the pupil, the lens further refracts the light rays, adjusting their focus to converge at a specific point on the retina, known as the focal point. This process allows for the formation of a clear image.

(A) Light from a distant source ✓

💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain how the lens of the eye contributes to vision.
Explanation: The lens of the eye refracts light rays that enter the eye, bending them so that they converge on the retina. This process allows the eye to focus on objects at various distances, enabling clear vision. When light from a distant source enters the eye, the lens adjusts its shape to ensure that the light rays meet at the retina, where the image is formed.

💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
How does the human eye focus light from a distant source?
