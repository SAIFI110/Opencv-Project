📝 Question 1: Short Question (سؤال قصير)
What type of energy does the tennis ball have at its highest point?
Answer: Potential energy (طاقة وضع).
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
What does the upward pointing arrow between the boy's hand and the ball represent?
(A) Kinetic energy (طاقة حركية) ✓
(B) Potential energy (طاقة وضع)
(C) Thermal energy (طاقة حرارية)
(D) Chemical energy (طاقة كيميائية)
Correct Option: (A) • The upward arrow indicates the kinetic energy of the ball as it moves upwards after being thrown.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain how the energy of the tennis ball changes as it moves upwards and then downwards.
Explanation: As the tennis ball moves upwards, its kinetic energy is converted into potential energy, reaching maximum potential energy at its highest point. When the ball begins to fall, the potential energy is converted back into kinetic energy, increasing as it descends.
Diagram #2 • diagram_page_113_diag_21.png 1360x680
📝 Question 1: Short Question (سؤال قصير)
What is the primary function of the turbine in the electricity generation process?
Answer: The primary function of the turbine is to convert the energy from steam, water, or wind into kinetic energy by rotating its blades.
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
What causes the turbine blades to rotate?
(A) The flow of steam, water, or wind between the blades ✓
(B) The electric current generated by the generator
(C) The weight of the turbine itself
(D) The magnetic field around the generator
Correct Option: (A) • The turbine blades rotate due to the high-speed flow of steam, water, or wind between them, which is indicated by the arrows in the diagram.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain how the rotation of the turbine is connected to electricity generation.
Explanation: The rotation of the turbine, caused by the flow of steam, water, or wind, turns a shaft that is connected to an electric generator. As the shaft rotates, it induces an electric current in the generator, converting mechanical energy into electrical energy.
Diagram #3 • diagram_page_114_diag_22.png 951x672
📝 Question 1: Short Question (سؤال قصير)
What type of energy is transformed in the boiler during the power generation process?
Answer: Chemical energy is transformed into thermal energy in the boiler.
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
What is the role of the turbine in the energy transformation process?
(A) It converts kinetic energy in steam into mechanical energy. ✓
(B) It stores chemical energy from natural gas.
(C) It generates thermal energy from water.
(D) It transports natural gas to the boiler.
Correct Option: (A) • The turbine converts the kinetic energy in steam into mechanical energy, which is essential for power generation.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain how chemical energy is converted into kinetic energy in this power generation process.
Explanation: In this process, chemical energy stored in natural gas is first released when the gas is burned in the boiler, producing thermal energy. This thermal energy heats water, converting it into steam. The steam then possesses kinetic energy, which is harnessed by the turbine to generate mechanical energy, ultimately producing electricity.
