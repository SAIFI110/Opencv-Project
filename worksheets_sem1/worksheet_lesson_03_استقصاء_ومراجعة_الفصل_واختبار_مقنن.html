What is the solubility of sodium chloride at 90°C?
Answer: Approximately 39 grams/100 grams of water.
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
Which substance shows a significant increase in solubility with rising temperature?
(A) كلوريد الصوديوم (Sodium Chloride)
(B) كلوريد البوتاسيوم (Potassium Chloride) ✓
(C) سكر (Sugar)
(D) ملح الطعام (Table Salt)
Correct Option: (B) • كلوريد البوتاسيوم (Potassium Chloride) shows a clear upward trend in solubility as temperature increases, unlike كلوريد الصوديوم (Sodium Chloride), which remains relatively flat.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain why the solubility of potassium chloride increases significantly with temperature compared to sodium chloride.
Explanation: The solubility of potassium chloride increases significantly with temperature due to its molecular structure and the interactions between its ions and water molecules. As temperature rises, the kinetic energy of water molecules increases, allowing them to interact more effectively with potassium chloride, breaking it down into ions and increasing its solubility. In contrast, sodium chloride has a more stable solubility profile, indicating that its dissolution is less affected by temperature changes.
