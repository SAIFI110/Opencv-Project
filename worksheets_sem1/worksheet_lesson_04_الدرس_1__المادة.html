💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain how the arrangement of spheres in the crystal structure contributes to its stability.
Explanation: The arrangement of larger and smaller spheres in the crystal structure creates a stable lattice. The larger spheres occupy the corners and face-centered positions, while the smaller spheres fill the octahedral voids. This close packing maximizes the attractive forces between the ions, leading to a stable structure. Additionally, the alternating sizes of the spheres help to minimize empty space, further enhancing stability.
