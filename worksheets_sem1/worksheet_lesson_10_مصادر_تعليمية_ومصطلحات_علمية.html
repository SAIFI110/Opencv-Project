📝 Question 1: Short Question (سؤال قصير)
What are the two types of particles found in the nucleus of an atom?
Answer: Protons and neutrons.
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
What charge do protons carry in an atom?
(A) Positive ✓
(B) Negative
(C) Neutral
(D) Variable
Correct Option: (A) • Protons are represented by orange spheres with a '+' sign, indicating they carry a positive electrical charge.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain how the arrangement of electrons affects the chemical properties of an atom.
Explanation: The arrangement of electrons in distinct energy levels or shells determines how an atom interacts with other atoms. Atoms with similar electron configurations tend to exhibit similar chemical properties. For example, elements in the same group of the periodic table have the same number of valence electrons, which influences their reactivity and bonding behavior.

📝 Question 1: Short Question (سؤال قصير)
What are the four main states of matter?
Answer: The four main states of matter are solid, liquid, gas, and plasma.
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
Which state of matter has a definite shape and volume?
(A) Solid ✓
(B) Liquid
(C) Gas
(D) Plasma
Correct Option: (A) • A solid has both a definite shape and a definite volume, as indicated in the diagram.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain why gases do not have a definite shape or volume.
Explanation: Gases do not have a definite shape or volume because their particles are far apart and move freely. This allows them to expand and fill the container they are in, taking the shape of that container.

📝 Question 1: Short Question (سؤال قصير)
What is the first step in the process of hearing an echo?
Answer: The first step is the production of sound.
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
What happens after sound collides with a solid surface?
(A) Sound reflects ✓
(B) Sound disappears
(C) Sound amplifies
(D) Sound travels faster
Correct Option: (A) • After sound collides with a solid surface, it reflects, which is the next step in the sequence.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain how an echo is produced.
Explanation: An echo is produced when sound is generated, travels through the air, collides with a solid surface, and then reflects back to the listener. This sequence allows the listener to hear the sound again after a short delay, which is perceived as an echo.

📝 Question 1: Short Question (سؤال قصير)
What element do both diamond and graphite consist of?
Answer: Carbon.
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
How are the atoms arranged in diamond according to the diagram?
(A) In cubic crystals ✓
(B) In layers
(C) In a random structure
(D) In a hexagonal pattern
Correct Option: (A) • The diagram states that the atoms in diamond are arranged in cubic crystals, which is the defining characteristic of its structure.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain the difference in atomic arrangement between diamond and graphite.
Explanation: Diamond has a cubic crystal structure where carbon atoms are arranged in a three-dimensional lattice, providing it with hardness. In contrast, graphite has a layered structure where carbon atoms are arranged in hexagonal layers, allowing the layers to slide over each other, which gives graphite its lubricating properties.
