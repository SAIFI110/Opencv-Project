💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain why the Sun is classified as a state of matter in the diagram.
Explanation: The Sun is classified as plasma, which is one of the four fundamental states of matter. Plasma consists of ionized gases with free-moving charged particles, which is characteristic of the Sun's composition and behavior.
