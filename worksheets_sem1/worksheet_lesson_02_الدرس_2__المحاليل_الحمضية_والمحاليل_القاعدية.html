📝 Question 1: Short Question (سؤال قصير)
What is the product formed when a hydrogen ion combines with a water molecule?
Answer: The product formed is a hydronium ion (H₃O⁺).
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
What does the chemical equation H⁺ + H₂O → H₃O⁺ represent?
(A) Formation of a hydronium ion ✓
(B) Formation of a hydrogen molecule
(C) Dissociation of water
(D) Formation of oxygen gas
Correct Option: (A) • The equation shows that a hydrogen ion (H⁺) combines with a water molecule (H₂O) to form a hydronium ion (H₃O⁺).
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain how a hydronium ion is formed from a hydrogen ion and a water molecule.
Explanation: A hydronium ion (H₃O⁺) is formed when a hydrogen ion (H⁺) interacts with a water molecule (H₂O). The hydrogen ion, which is positively charged, combines with the water molecule, which consists of one oxygen atom and two hydrogen atoms. This interaction results in the addition of the hydrogen ion to the water molecule, creating the hydronium ion, which has three hydrogen atoms and retains a positive charge.

📝 Question 1: Short Question (سؤال قصير)
What pH value indicates a neutral substance?
Answer: A pH value of 7 indicates a neutral substance.
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
Which substance has the lowest pH value on the scale?
(A) Hydrochloric acid ✓
(B) Vinegar
(C) Stomach contents
(D) Milk
Correct Option: (A) • Hydrochloric acid has the lowest pH value of 0, indicating it is extremely acidic.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain why substances with a pH below 7 are considered acidic.
Explanation: Substances with a pH below 7 have a higher concentration of hydrogen ions (H+) compared to hydroxide ions (OH-), which results in acidic properties. The lower the pH value, the greater the acidity, as it reflects an increasing number of hydrogen ions in the solution.

📝 Question 1: Short Question (سؤال قصير)
What type of acid is represented in the left beaker?
Answer: Hydrochloric acid (حمض الهيدروكلوريك), which is a strong acid.
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
Which ion is present in a higher concentration in the hydrochloric acid solution compared to the ethanoic acid solution?
(A) Hydronium ion (أيون الهيدرونيوم) ✓
(B) Acetate ion (أيون الأسيتات)
(C) Chloride ion (أيون الكلوريد)
(D) Sodium ion (أيون الصوديوم)
Correct Option: (A) • The hydrochloric acid solution has a higher concentration of hydronium ions due to its strong acid nature, which fully ionizes in water.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain why hydrochloric acid is considered a strong acid while ethanoic acid is considered a weak acid.
Explanation: Hydrochloric acid is considered a strong acid because it completely ionizes in water, resulting in a high concentration of free ions, specifically hydronium and chloride ions. In contrast, ethanoic acid only partially ionizes in water, leading to a lower concentration of free ions, which is characteristic of weak acids.

Answer: The pH range for acidic solutions is from 0 to 7.
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
Which ion is predominantly found in acidic solutions?
(A) H₃O⁺ ✓
(B) OH⁻
(C) Na⁺
(D) Cl⁻
Correct Option: (A) • H₃O⁺ (hydronium ion) is the dominant ion in acidic solutions, as indicated on the left side of the pH scale in the diagram.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain how the pH scale indicates the acidity or basicity of a solution.
Explanation: The pH scale ranges from 0 to 14, where values below 7 indicate acidity (higher concentration of H₃O⁺ ions), values above 7 indicate basicity (higher concentration of OH⁻ ions), and a value of 7 indicates neutrality. The scale is logarithmic, meaning each whole number change represents a tenfold change in acidity or basicity.
