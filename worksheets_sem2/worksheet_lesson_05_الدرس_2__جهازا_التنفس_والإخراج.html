📝 Question 1: Short Question (سؤال قصير)
What is the process called when oxygen enters the body?
Answer: Inhalation (شهيق)
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
What happens to oxygen after it enters the lungs according to the diagram?
(A) It moves to the cells. ✓
(B) It is exhaled immediately.
(C) It is converted to carbon dioxide.
(D) It is stored in the lungs.
Correct Option: (A) • The diagram shows that oxygen moves from the lungs to the cells via the circulatory system.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain how the circulatory system is involved in the process of respiration.
Explanation: The circulatory system transports oxygen from the lungs to the body's cells using red blood cells. After inhalation, oxygen enters the lungs and is picked up by red blood cells, which then carry it to the cells throughout the body. This process is essential for cellular respiration, where oxygen is used to produce energy.

📝 Question 1: Short Question (سؤال قصير)
What is the primary function of the alveoli in the respiratory system?
Answer: The primary function of the alveoli is to facilitate gas exchange, allowing oxygen to enter the blood and carbon dioxide to be expelled.
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
What is the approximate number of alveoli in each lung?
(A) 300 million ✓
(B) 150 million
(C) 600 million
(D) 1 billion
Correct Option: (A) • The diagram states that there are approximately 300 million alveoli in each lung, which is crucial for efficient gas exchange.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain how the structure of the alveoli aids in gas exchange.
Explanation: The alveoli are tiny air sacs with thin walls that provide a large surface area for gas exchange. Their proximity to capillaries allows oxygen to diffuse into the blood while carbon dioxide diffuses out, making the process efficient.

📝 Question 1: Short Question (سؤال قصير)
What is the role of the diaphragm during inhalation?
Answer: The diaphragm contracts and moves downward, increasing the thoracic cavity volume and allowing air to enter the lungs.
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
Which structure is indicated by the downward-pointing blue arrow that shows air entering the body?
(A) Trachea ✓
(B) Lungs
(C) Diaphragm
(D) Rib cage
Correct Option: (A) • The trachea is the airway that allows air to enter the lungs, as indicated by the downward-pointing blue arrow in the diagram.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain how the diaphragm contributes to the process of inhalation.
Explanation: During inhalation, the diaphragm contracts and moves downward, which increases the volume of the thoracic cavity. This decrease in pressure allows air to flow into the lungs from the atmosphere, facilitating the exchange of gases necessary for respiration.

📝 Question 1: Short Question (سؤال قصير)
What is the Arabic term for exhalation as shown in the diagram?
Answer: زفير (zafir)
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
What happens to the diaphragm during exhalation?
(A) It moves upwards. ✓
(B) It moves downwards.
(C) It remains stationary.
(D) It expands outward.
Correct Option: (A) • During exhalation, the diaphragm moves upwards, which helps push air out of the lungs.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain how the process of exhalation occurs in the human body.
Explanation: Exhalation occurs when the diaphragm contracts and moves upwards, reducing the volume of the chest cavity. This decrease in volume increases the pressure inside the lungs, causing air to flow out of the lungs and through the trachea. The contraction of the lungs, indicated by the arrows in the diagram, also aids in pushing air out, resulting in the expulsion of carbon dioxide from the body.

💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain how the digestive system and excretory system are interconnected in the human body.
Explanation: The digestive system breaks down food into nutrients, which are absorbed into the bloodstream. The excretory system, particularly the kidneys, filters out waste products from the blood, including those derived from the digestion of food. This interconnectedness ensures that the body maintains a balance of nutrients and eliminates waste efficiently.

💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain the role of nephrons in the kidney.
Explanation: Nephrons are the functional units of the kidney responsible for filtering blood, removing waste, and regulating water and electrolyte balance. Each nephron consists of a glomerulus and a tubule system that processes the filtrate to form urine, which is then excreted from the body.
