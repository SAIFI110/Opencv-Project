📝 Question 1: Short Question (سؤال قصير)
What is the role of B-cells in the adaptive immune response?
Answer: B-cells produce antibodies that help neutralize pathogens.
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
What is the first step in the adaptive immune response as shown in the diagram?
(A) Identification of the pathogen ✓
(B) Activation of B-cells
(C) Production of antibodies
(D) Destruction of pathogens
Correct Option: (A) • The diagram illustrates that the first step involves white blood cells identifying and engulfing the pathogen.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain how helper T-cells contribute to the activation of B-cells.
Explanation: Helper T-cells, once activated by the engulfed pathogen, release signals that stimulate B-cells to proliferate and differentiate into plasma cells, which produce antibodies specific to the pathogen.

💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain why the staining of bacteria is important in microbiology.
Explanation: Staining bacteria is crucial in microbiology because it enhances the visibility of the bacteria under a microscope, allowing for better identification and classification. Different staining techniques, such as Gram staining, can also provide information about the bacterial cell wall structure, which is essential for determining the appropriate treatment for bacterial infections.

Answer: The medium used is blood agar.
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
What is the purpose of the quadrant streaking technique used in the petri dish?
(A) To isolate individual bacterial colonies ✓
(B) To increase the growth rate of bacteria
(C) To measure the pH of the medium
(D) To identify the color of the bacteria
Correct Option: (A) • The quadrant streaking technique is specifically designed to isolate individual bacterial colonies from a mixed sample.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain how the appearance of bacterial colonies can indicate the effectiveness of the agar medium used.
Explanation: The appearance of bacterial colonies, such as their color, size, and edge characteristics, can indicate the effectiveness of the agar medium. For instance, blood agar allows for the growth of various bacteria and can show hemolytic activity, which is evident in the colony morphology. If the colonies are well-defined and varied, it suggests that the medium supports diverse bacterial growth, making it effective for culturing microorganisms.
