💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain the trend of Ahmed's height as he ages from 0 to 18 years.
Explanation: Ahmed's height generally increases with age, as indicated by the upward trend of the blue line on the graph. The growth is relatively steady during the early years (0-12 years), showing a consistent increase in height. 📝 Question 1: Short Question (سؤال قصير)
What are the two types of particles found in the nucleus of an atom?
Answer: Protons and neutrons.
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
How many protons are shown in the nucleus of the atom in the diagram?
(A) 7 ✓
(B) 5
(C) 12
(D) 10
Correct Option: (A) • The diagram clearly shows 7 orange spheres labeled as protons in the nucleus.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain the role of electrons in an atom.
Explanation: Electrons are negatively charged particles that orbit the nucleus of an atom. They are responsible for chemical bonding and reactions, as they can be gained, lost, or shared between atoms. The arrangement of electrons in different energy levels determines the atom's reactivity and the types of bonds it can form.

📝 Question 1: Short Question (سؤال قصير)
What are the four main states of matter?
Answer: Solid, Liquid, Gas, and Plasma.
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
Which state of matter has a definite shape and volume?
(A) Solid ✓
(B) Liquid
(C) Gas
(D) Plasma
Correct Option: (A) • Solids have both a definite shape and a definite volume, as indicated in the diagram.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain why gases do not have a definite shape or volume.
Explanation: Gases consist of particles that are far apart and move freely. This allows them to fill the shape of their container and expand to occupy any available volume, resulting in no definite shape or volume.

📝 Question 1: Short Question (سؤال قصير)
What are the two forms of carbon illustrated in the Venn diagram?
Answer: Diamond (ألماس) and graphite (الجرافيت).
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
What arrangement of atoms is characteristic of diamond?
(A) Atoms are arranged in the form of cubic crystals. ✓
(B) Atoms are arranged in layers.
(C) Atoms are arranged randomly.
(D) Atoms are arranged in a hexagonal pattern.
Correct Option: (A) • The Venn diagram states that in diamond, atoms are arranged in the form of cubic crystals.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain why both diamond and graphite are considered forms of carbon.
Explanation: Both diamond and graphite are composed of carbon atoms, which is indicated in the intersection of the Venn diagram. Despite their different structures—diamond having a cubic crystal arrangement and graphite having a layered arrangement—they share the same elemental composition, making them both forms of carbon.
