📝 Question 1: Short Question (سؤال قصير)
What is the function of cartilage in long bones?
Answer: Cartilage covers the articular surfaces of bones, providing a smooth surface for joint movement and cushioning.
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
Which structure is located at the ends of the long bone?
(A) الغضروف (Cartilage) ✓
(B) العظم الكثيف (Compact Bone)
(C) تجويف النخاع (Medullary Cavity)
(D) السمحاق (Periosteum)
Correct Option: (A) • The cartilage (الغضروف) is specifically located at the ends of the long bone, covering the articular surfaces.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain how the structure of spongy bone contributes to its function.
Explanation: Spongy bone has a porous, lattice-like structure that reduces the weight of the bone while providing strength and support. This design allows for the efficient distribution of forces and helps in the production of blood cells within the bone marrow located in the medullary cavity.

📝 Question 1: Short Question (سؤال قصير)
What is the function of the cornea in the human eye?
Answer: The cornea is the transparent outer layer that helps to focus light entering the eye.
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
Which part of the eye is responsible for collecting light before it reaches the retina?
(A) العدسة (Lens) ✓
(B) البؤبؤ (Pupil)
(C) القزحية (Iris)
(D) القرنية (Cornea)
Correct Option: (A) • The lens (العدسة) focuses light onto the retina, allowing for clear vision.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain how light travels through the eye and its importance for vision.
Explanation: Light enters the eye through the cornea, which bends the light rays. It then passes through the pupil, where the iris controls the amount of light entering. The lens further focuses the light onto the retina, where photoreceptors convert the light into electrical signals sent to the brain via the optic nerve. This process is crucial for forming clear images.

📝 Question 1: Short Question (سؤال قصير)
What are the three main parts of the human ear?
Answer: The three main parts of the human ear are the outer ear, middle ear, and inner ear.
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
Which structure separates the outer ear from the middle ear?
(A) Eardrum (غشاء الطبلة) ✓
(B) Cochlea (القوقعة)
(C) Pinna (الصيوان)
(D) Auditory Canal (القناة السمعية)
Correct Option: (A) • The eardrum (غشاء الطبلة) is the membrane that separates the outer ear from the middle ear.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain how sound travels through the different parts of the ear.
Explanation: Sound waves enter the outer ear through the pinna and travel down the auditory canal to the eardrum, causing it to vibrate. These vibrations are transmitted to the ossicles in the middle ear (malleus, incus, and stapes), which amplify the sound. The stapes then transfers these vibrations to the cochlea in the inner ear, where they are converted into electrical signals that are sent to the brain for interpretation.

📝 Question 1: Short Question (سؤال قصير)
What is the function of taste hairs in a taste bud?
Answer: Taste hairs, or microvilli, extend into the taste pore and are responsible for detecting dissolved substances, allowing us to taste.
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
Which part of the taste bud allows dissolved substances to enter?
(A) ثقب ذوقي (Taste pore) ✓
(B) شعيرات ذوقية (Taste hairs)
(C) خلايا حسية (Sensory cells)
(D) خلايا داعمة (Supporting cells)
Correct Option: (A) • The taste pore (ثقب ذوقي) is the opening at the top of the taste bud where dissolved substances can enter, making it essential for the taste sensation.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain how the sensory nerve fiber is connected to the taste bud.
Explanation: The sensory nerve fiber (ليف عصبي حسي) extends from the base of the taste bud, connecting it to the nervous system. This connection allows the taste bud to send signals to the brain when it detects taste stimuli, facilitating the perception of taste.
