📝 Question 1: Short Question (سؤال قصير)
What is the function of the liver in the digestive system?
Answer: The liver produces bile, which helps in the digestion and absorption of fats.
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
Which organ is responsible for producing saliva in the digestive system?
(A) غدد لعابية (Ghudud Lu'abiyah) ✓
(B) معدة (Mi'dah)
(C) كبد (Kibid)
(D) مريء (Marii')
Correct Option: (A) • The salivary glands (غدد لعابية) produce saliva, which begins the process of digestion in the mouth.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain how the digestive system processes food after it is chewed.
Explanation: After food is chewed, it is mixed with saliva and formed into a bolus, which is then swallowed and travels down the esophagus (مريء) to the stomach (معدة). In the stomach, food is mixed with gastric juices for further digestion before moving to the small intestine (أمعاء دقيقة) for nutrient absorption.

📝 Question 1: Short Question (سؤال قصير)
What is the function of the salivary glands in the human mouth?
Answer: The salivary glands produce saliva, which aids in digestion and helps in the formation of food boluses.
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
How much saliva do the salivary glands produce daily?
(A) 1.5 liters ✓
(B) 2 liters
(C) 0.5 liters
(D) 3 liters
Correct Option: (A) • The salivary glands produce approximately 1.5 liters of saliva daily, as stated in the diagram.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain how saliva contributes to the digestive process.
Explanation: Saliva contains enzymes that begin the breakdown of carbohydrates, moistens food for easier swallowing, and helps in taste perception. It also has antibacterial properties that protect the mouth and throat.

📝 Question 1: Short Question (سؤال قصير)
What is the primary function of the villi in the small intestine?
Answer: The primary function of the villi is to increase the surface area for absorption of nutrients.
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
What do the red and blue colors in the villus represent?
(A) Arterial and venous capillaries ✓
(B) Lymphatic vessels
(C) Nerve endings
(D) Muscle fibers
Correct Option: (A) • The red and blue colors indicate arterial and venous capillaries within the villus, which are responsible for blood flow.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain how the structure of the villi aids in nutrient absorption in the small intestine.
Explanation: The structure of the villi, with their large surface area and presence of capillaries, allows for efficient nutrient absorption. The folds increase the surface area, while the capillaries transport absorbed nutrients directly into the bloodstream, facilitating quick distribution throughout the body.

📝 Question 1: Short Question (سؤال قصير)
What are adipocytes primarily responsible for storing?
Answer: Adipocytes are primarily responsible for storing fat.
🔘 Question 2: Multiple Choice (اختيار من متعدد) 🔍 Diagram-Based
Which component of the adipocyte is indicated by the label 'دهون مختزنة'?
(A) Stored fat ✓
(B) Nucleus
(C) Cytoplasm
(D) Interstitial tissue
Correct Option: (A) • The label 'دهون مختزنة' directly translates to 'Stored fat', which refers to the large, clear areas within the adipocytes.
💡 Question 3: Scientific Reasoning (سؤال تعليلي)
Explain why adipocytes have a flattened nucleus.
Explanation: Adipocytes have a flattened nucleus because the large fat droplet occupies most of the cell's interior space, pushing the nucleus to the periphery. This adaptation allows for maximum storage of fat within the cell.
